Every intentional Lab deviation from the frozen demo gets a dated row. A divergence "graduates" only when PRD.md or DESIGN.md is amended to match — until then it ships nowhere.

| 2026-07-28c | **Inverted to light, and the avatar grows ears.** White page, dark cards, dark CTA with a light label; the identity ramp flips into the dark half with a WHITE initial inside each token. `face()` gains two ears, turning the head from the old monk into a rodent — same head, same `--fs` ratios, same Nose on the same `--notch` scale. | Asked for: black and white interchanged, so the app reads mostly white. Done by re-deriving ROLES rather than flipping luminance: a mechanical flip inverts values but not meanings, and it turned `line` (a hairline) near-white and invisible, and pushed `private` (the secret card) the wrong way — "recessed" and "hairline" are defined relative to the page, so when the page moves they must be re-reasoned, not negated. | 2026-07-28b | **Monochrome, and a rat for a mark.** One greyscale ramp replaces the whole palette — chrome, the eight player identities, and all three board themes (desaturated by LUMINANCE, so every value relationship survives and only hue is removed). | 2026-07-28 | **The countdown becomes a bar, and the last 15 seconds accelerate.** The 44 px conic ring is replaced by a full-width depleting bar (`scaleX` on the compositor, `transform` never `width`) that thickens 8 → 10 → 13 px through warn and urgent. New pure `clockPulseHz()` in `clock.js` and `TIMERS.PULSE_MS = 15000`: the siren now opens at 15 s rather than 5 and its period is driven by a rate that climbs 0.8 → 2.5 Hz. Two edge-triggered beats — a soft `closing` tone + short `closing` tap at 15 s, the existing `urgent` + `.warning` at 5 s. The clock is now on in every mode **except** one-phone. New fixture support: `FX_NOW` freezes `Date.now()` under `?fixture=`, so screen 09 poses with a live deadline. | Asked for directly. The bar also fixes something the ring hid: `ckPaint` only writes when the displayed integer second changes, and a circle losing 1/60th of a turn is invisible where a full-width bar stepping the same amount reads as blinking (game-feel principle 7) — so the glide is a 1 s CSS transition rather than a faster tick. The rate climbs because acceleration is information a fixed rate cannot carry: 330 ms says "urgent", a rising beat says "and getting worse". **Capped at 2.5 Hz because WCAG 2.3.1 puts the photosensitive-seizure threshold at three flashes per second** — a safety limit, not a taste one, and the reason extra pressure must go to sound or haptics instead. The inaction gate is inherited unchanged from the siren: it fires only where the player still owes an answer, because telling someone who already submitted that they are out of time is a false claim about their own state. The mode rule was also a real bug — keyed on `party()`, the clock was silently **off** in online rooms, since online sets `NET.kind` and not `U.mode`, so the mode that most needs a deadline was the one running without one. Fixtures had `timers.on = false` everywhere for determinism, which left the countdown the one component nobody could review; freezing the clock fixes the cause rather than the symptom. | Graduated (DESIGN §7, amended 2026-07-28) |
| 2026-07-27b | **The door stays open all game, and the waiting room stops lying.** `netJoinOpen()` drops the 3-minute clock — only a finished game refuses (`LATE_JOIN_MS` and `G.joinOpenUntil` retired). The queue is not a compromise — a new chair genuinely cannot appear mid-round, because `gmForRound()`, `winCheck()` and `scoreRound()` all read `playerCount`, so the round boundary is the only safe moment; the waiting room the player experiences and the invariant the engine needs are the same rule. | 2026-07-27 | **The last-five-seconds siren.** Inside `TIMERS.URGENT_MS` the screen edges pulse `bluffPink` on a 330 ms half-cycle sine, with one soft falling two-tone and one `warning` haptic on entry.
